🎓 APP: Advanced statistics in education

Nov 3, 2026

Education is where many of my methods meet real data. With the Adaptive Experimentation Accelerator, winner of the 2023 XPRIZE Digital Learning Challenge, we run bandit-driven adaptive experiments in real online courses; a Sapienza research grant (PI, 2025) now brings these methods into our own statistics courses. With ISTAT, I develop scalable methods to measure the accuracy of education statistics built from multisource registers. And with colleagues at Sapienza, we model students’ careers, for example to understand late graduations.

Team: Joseph Jay Williams (Intelligent Adaptive Interventions Lab, University of Toronto), Anna Rafferty, Audrey Durand, and colleagues at Carnegie Mellon University and North Carolina State University; Piero Demetrio Falorsi, Stefano Falorsi, Diego Chianella and Giorgio Alleva ( ISTAT); Brunero Liseo, Andrea Tancredi and Edoardo Pandolfo (Sapienza).

Where to start: Platform-based Adaptive Experimental Research in Education: Lessons Learned from Winning a Digital Learning Challenge (LAK25, 2025) and Scalable Generalised Accuracy Estimation for Multisource Register-based Official Statistics (JRSS-A, 2026).
