Thesis supervision

I supervise Bachelor’s and Master’s theses at Sapienza, mainly for students of the degrees where I teach (Economics and Finance; Finance and Insurance). Theses can be methodological, applied, or a mix of the two, and usually involve working with real data in R.

Before writing to me, please read the 📘 Thesis Vademecum. It explains how I supervise and what I expect from thesis students.

Possible topics. Topics are agreed together, starting from your interests and the courses you took. Typical areas are:

  • statistical inference and statistical learning (estimation, testing, regression, classification, resampling);
  • time series and financial time series (ARIMA and GARCH models, forecasting, volatility);
  • uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction;
  • adaptive experiments, bandits and reinforcement learning for decision making;
  • applications to economics, finance, health and education.

See Research Interests for the projects I am currently working on.

How to express interest. Send an email to nina.deliu [at] uniroma1 [dot] it with subject “Thesis request – [BSc/MSc] – Your Name”, including:

  1. your degree programme, year, and matriculation number;
  2. the topic or area you are interested in (even roughly);
  3. the graduation session you are aiming for.

Please write well in advance of your intended graduation session, so that there is enough time to define the topic and do the work properly.
